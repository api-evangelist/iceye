The Esri User Conference is a pinnacle event for the global geospatial community, and this year’s gathering in San Diego was no exception. With 20,000 professionals converging from across the globe, the energy on the show floor was palpable. For the Solutions team at ICEYE, it was an incredible opportunity to connect with partners and customers who rely on our SAR-derived data to make critical, real-world decisions.
