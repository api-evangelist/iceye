For most of its history, ICEYE has answered one question for the insurance industry : what is happening. A flood recedes, a wildfire moves through a region, a hurricane makes landfall, and within hours ICEYE's satellites deliver a picture of the damage that adjusters and underwriters can act on immediately. That question is still central to the business.
